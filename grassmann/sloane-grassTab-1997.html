N. J. A. Sloane: Table of Best Grassmannian Packings

Gives best packings known of N n-spaces in m-space.

Based on joint work with A. R. Calderbank, J. H. Conway, R. H. Hardin, E. M. Rains, P. W. Shor and others.

  • Grassmannian packings home page

  • The following table gives the best packing known to us of N n-dimensional subspaces in ordinary real Euclidean m-space.

  • The case n=1, m=3 thus corresponds to the best way to separate N laser beams passing through the center of an ordinary sphere.

  • The entry gives the largest known minimal squared distance D.

  • For n=1 this is defined to be sin(theta)^2, where theta is the minimal angle between any two of the beams.

  • In general the squared distance between two n-spaces is the sum of the squares of the sines of the n principal angles between the two spaces.

  • The table is in two sections. The first section gives packings found (mostly) by computer, the second gives packings constructed (mostly) by hand.

  • In the lower dimensions the packings labeled with file names are the best found after very extensive computer searches. But for the higher-dimensional packings and for larger values of N only a few runs were made, in order to have a rough standard by which to judge other constructions.
