# Eighteen cells the E8 lattice had already won

*Abridged summary of the model's reasoning, row m = 8, n = 1 (not in the catalogue).*

The row for lines in R^8 has blank cells for N = 101..120. The search ran on 18 of them and its best value was 0.749454.

Every one was rejected. The E8 root system has 240 roots, which give 120 lines. Two roots of E8 have inner product 0 or ±1 with norm 2, so two of these lines meet at 90° or 60°, and the squared chordal distance (the squared sine of the angle) is at least 3/4. Any N ≤ 120 of these lines therefore already reach 0.75. The table lists the 120-line value as 0.7499999999.

A number found by search that is below what you get by deleting lines from a known configuration is not a result. The counting rule (strictly better than the best value for any larger N in the row) exists for this case, and it caught all 18 cells without anyone looking at the row. The same rule rejected 141 cells in total, most of them in rows m = 16 and m = 9, where large special constructions are known.
